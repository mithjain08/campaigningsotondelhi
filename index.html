The Ticket

Three people, one plan for how the council should run.

Each of us is standing for a different job, but we built one shared plan — so a vote for any one of us is a vote for all three working together.

01
Vice President

Aryav

As Vice President, Aryav will support the President in leading the council, represent students directly in council and administration meetings, oversee committee work, and step in to lead whenever the President can't.

"Your VOICE - Your VICE PRESIDENT."

02
Secretary

Ved

As Secretary, Ved will keep official records and meeting minutes, handle communication between the council and students, manage scheduling, and make sure every council decision is documented and easy to find.

"B1"
In Ved's words"I'm going to make you an offer you can't refuse..."

Treasurer

Mith

As Treasurer, Mith will manage the council's budget, track and approve funding for student events, keep spending transparent and reported to students, and lead fundraising for council initiatives.

"Money follows my brother, money follows... "
